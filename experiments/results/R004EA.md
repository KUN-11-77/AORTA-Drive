## Question and falsification

R004E-A asked whether the already-qualified polygon teacher could be instantiated on
the exact frozen R004 train/development anchors without reading calibration, source test
or target-domain samples. A pre-freeze exact-token join rejected direct reuse of the
R002G artifacts: none of its 164 scene tokens occurred among the 1440 R004
train/development anchors. The failed join is retained as evidence and no R002G label was
substituted by scene proximity or file order.

An independent one-anchor regeneration also falsified raw compressed-pickle equality as
a replay condition. The two raw cache SHA-256 values differed, while the canonical hash
of the exact teacher inputs and the final label artifact SHA-256 were identical. The
frozen replay contract therefore records raw pickle hashes as provenance, requires
semantic replay of rear-axle pose and selected polygon rings, and still requires
byte-identical label artifacts.

## Staged qualification

The execution ladder was completed without changing the data definition:

1. One anchor: identical semantic teacher hash and byte-identical label artifact across
   two independent cache builds; raw pickle bytes intentionally did not match.
2. Eight complete recording groups: 128/128 cache records and 128/128 label records;
   every group contained exactly 16 anchors and all 128 label artifacts were reopened by
   the independent audit.
3. Full train/development set: 90 recording groups, 519 source log fragments and
   1440/1440 exact anchors. The label builder produced 5,898,240 node labels on the frozen
   64x64 grid with zero reported failures.

The full independent audit reopened all 1440 label artifacts and found:

- zero missing, extra, malformed, nonfinite or negative-error-ledger arrays;
- exactly 90 groups with 16 anchors per group;
