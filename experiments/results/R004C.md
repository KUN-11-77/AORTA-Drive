## What was tested

R004C froze a planner-independent challenger library that consumes only the norm of the current ego velocity. It does not read a map, road or trajectory GT, paired clean target, learned field, certificate, planner score, calibration residual, or target-domain sample. Native remains mandatory and is added separately as $\tau_0$.

Each challenger uses one of 35 ordered constant-acceleration/constant-curvature controls over 4 s at 0.5 s intervals. Arc motion is integrated analytically in traveled distance; braking stops at zero velocity and never reverses. Controls above 20 m/s, 3 m/s² absolute longitudinal acceleration, or 4 m/s² lateral acceleration are rejected before use.

The clean replay covered all 1440 train/development anchors from 90 recording groups and 519 logs. Every candidate batch was generated twice and required exact pose-array replay.

## Results

- Source initial speed ranged from $7.80\times10^{-5}$ to 13.90 m/s; median was 4.66 m/s.
- Every anchor retained 23–35 dynamically eligible challengers; mean was 33.55.
- Every anchor retained at least 5 candidates whose rear-axle samples remained inside the frozen ±32 m ROI; mean was 30.78 and maximum was 35.
- Dynamic filtering rejected 2082 anchor-control instances for lateral acceleration. No accepted control violated the speed or longitudinal-acceleration envelope.
- No map, label, image, native trajectory, planner output, or score was used to construct or filter the library.

## Pre-freeze falsification and repair

The first train/development audit used accelerations `[0, -1, -2, +1]` m/s². It found 12/1440 high-speed anchors with no challenger whose rear-axle samples stayed inside the certificate ROI. Before protocol freeze, the provider added a $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ emergency-deceleration row and explicitly fixed the longitudinal envelope at 3 m/s². The clean replay then removed all reference-point-empty anchors without consulting road labels, planner outcomes, calibration residuals, or target data.

## Claim boundary and next blocker

Rear-axle samples inside the ROI are only an early rejection check, not a full-vehicle swept-footprint certificate. Every candidate used later must still pass the frozen asymmetric geometry checker. R004C also does not evaluate meaningful non-inclusion, GT road-margin improvement, selection utility, planner transfer, or candidate quality relative to native.

R004A–C now freeze the split/time set, visual projection/missing evidence, and candidate supply. The remaining pre-calibration blocker is to freeze a legal source-only predictor architecture, training objective, and checkpoint rule using train/development only. Calibration residuals remain unread.

The prescribed independent cross-model integrity reviewer was unavailable in the current tool environment. No self-review was substituted as an acquittal; the result remains a deterministic protocol audit with the narrow claim boundary above.
