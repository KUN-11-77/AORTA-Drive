post-result mechanism diagnosis reproduced the same eight fit-only sentinels and 48 frozen

## Theory consequence

For outer rear-axle radius `R`, yaw-invariant inner radius `r`, grid-cell radius `h` and
common pose-error radius `epsilon`, any inner-cell center lies at most
`r - epsilon - h` from a trajectory sample. The matching sample of the other trajectory is
contained in an incident segment disk. By the triangle inequality, neither direction can
produce a witness whenever every matched rear-axle separation is at most

`R - r + 2 h + 2 epsilon`.

The implementation rounds separation upward and the threshold downward. This is a
sufficient condition only; no converse is claimed.

The next geometry architecture must retain vehicle orientation. A candidate design is a
mid-pose oriented rectangle dilated by certified translation, yaw and pose-error terms,
paired with full-cell interior witnesses. Before registration it still needs interval-safe
trigonometry, outward distance comparisons, ROI failure semantics and adversarial tests.
