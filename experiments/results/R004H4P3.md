## New interface finding

The numerical STOP does **not** yet establish that the native-conditioned proposal family is
physically unusable in NAVSIM. R004H4P3 screened a self-constructed C2 interpolation of the
desired 0.5-second poses. The official scorer instead converts desired poses to an
`InterpolatedTrajectory`, resamples native and prediction at 0.1 seconds, and applies
`BatchLQRTracker` plus `BatchKinematicBicycleModel` before scoring the simulated states.

Therefore the object rejected 590 times is not the trajectory consumed by the official
scorer. The same mismatch also affects prior intent and footprint checks, which compared raw
desired trajectories rather than paired simulated native/challenger states.
