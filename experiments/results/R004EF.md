# R004E-F Result — Field signal versus tail failure

## Verdict

**TAIL/OBJECTIVE MISMATCH SUPPORTED in 24/24 development groups. Calibration remains
sealed.** The frozen visual field contains useful average edge information, but its
sparse worst errors invalidate the current uniform whole-grid maximum interface.

| System | Mean of group-mean edge MAE | Worst-5 group maximum | All-24 group maximum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Zero field | `0.554261445 m` | `1.000000954 m` | `1.000000725 m` |
| Frozen visual | `0.494434237 m` | `1.475400233 m` | `1.394614086 m` |
| Sign-flipped visual | `0.638136041 m` | `1.847584558 m` | `1.650731449 m` |

The visual field reduced mean edge MAE by `10.79%` relative to zero, and it did so in
all 24 independent recording groups. Yet its exact group maximum was worse than zero
in all 24 groups. Every group had positive predicted/teacher edge cosine
(`0.4408–0.6632`), while sign flipping degraded both central and tail endpoints.
Therefore the failure is neither a global sign convention bug nor complete absence of
transferable visual signal.

For teacher edges with `|D label| >= 0.9 m`, within-group sign accuracy ranged from
`0.7748` to `0.8987`. The remaining sparse wrong-direction high-gradient edges are
enough to dominate the whole-grid maximum despite useful average alignment.

## Theory and architecture consequence

The next version must not merely enlarge the potential decoder. It needs an
observation-conditioned, candidate-independent **edge uncertainty shape** `s_e(X)` and
a session-level standardized residual event

\[
\max_{t,e}\frac{|(Du_\theta-D\widetilde d)_e|}{s_e(X)} \le q_\alpha.
\]

This keeps simultaneous source-session coverage while allowing the min-plus graph
metric to charge large cost only to unreliable edges instead of applying one uniform
worst-edge penalty everywhere. The scale head, group-aligned objective, anti-inflation
constraint and exact theorem must be frozen before any new training. R004E-F does not
show that such a scale is learnable or that its certificates are nontrivial.
