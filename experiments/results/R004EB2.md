## Question and representation

R004E-B2 asked whether every frozen R004 train/development anchor can be given
replayable visual evidence that is exactly joined to its R004E-A teacher without storing
the approximately 169 GiB redundant node-slot tensor.

For anchor (a), slot (s) and graph node (q), the training evidence is

\[
X_{a,q,s}=m_{a,q,s}\,\mathcal B(F_{a,s},\pi_{a,q,s}),
\]

where (F) is the frozen normalized last-four-layer DINO map, \(\pi\) is the frozen
distortion-aware patch coordinate, (m) is the explicit support mask and \(\mathcal B\)
is bilinear interpolation over patch centers. R004E-B stores the exact BF16 bit pattern
of (F), float64 \(\pi\) and Boolean (m); it introduces no learned projection, PCA,
lossy quantization, padding or clamping.

## Staged qualification

The unchanged protocol passed all three stages:

1. **EB0, one anchor:** two independent builds produced byte-identical dense/projection
   artifacts and the same logical manifest; the sampler oracle error was
   `1.9073486328125e-6`.
2. **EB1, eight complete groups:** 128 anchors and 1422 unique image features passed exact
   feature-label join and complete artifact reopening; sampler error was
   `3.814697265625e-6`.
3. **EB2, all 90 groups:** 1440 anchors and 17,133 unique image features passed the full
   independent audit described below.

## Full results

- exact source scope: 90 recording groups, 1440 anchors and 17,280 slot references;
- content-addressed cache: 17,133 unique raw-image hashes, with no merging of distinct
  image bytes;
- 17,133/17,133 dense feature artifacts reopened with zero hash, shape or finiteness
  failure;
- 2880/2880 projection arrays reopened with zero hash, dtype, shape or mask-contract
  failure;
- 1440/1440 feature records matched the frozen R004E-A label manifest by exact
  `(log_name, scene_token)`; no missing, extra or duplicate anchor was accepted;
- one anchor per recording group was independently materialized on 64 deterministic
  nodes; NumPy versus torch sampling differed by at most
  `4.0531158447265625e-6 < 1e-5`;
- invalid slots were exact zero and all sampled values were finite;
- 2,452,045 anchor-node instances were all-missing, exactly reproducing the R004B
  projection audit count and its 41.5725% missing fraction;
- artifact size was 26,469,439,104 bytes (24.65 GiB), below the frozen 40 GiB limit;
- generation took 1255.08 seconds and peak GPU allocation was 2,034,846,720 bytes on the
  RTX 3090;
- calibration, source-test and target-domain samples were not accessed.

## Integrity boundary

The independent executable audit did not trust generation summaries: it reopened the
actual cache files, recomputed their hashes, checked array contents and independently
joined the frozen labels. The prescribed external cross-model experiment reviewer was not
available in the current tool environment, so no self-review is presented as an external
acquittal.

## Claim boundary and next blocker

R004E-B closes only the replayable visual feature/projection/label interface and its
full source train/development coverage. It does not establish learned prediction accuracy,
calibration coverage, certificate sharpness, candidate utility, planner improvement, OOD
robustness, real-time training/inference or Gate B success.

R004E-C must now freeze the data loader, optimizer, schedule, checkpoint ledger and
development-group selection rule, run a small overfit/gradient sanity gate, and only then
train the single registered quotient-field probe. Calibration residuals remain unread.
