# R004H4P2S cached-native speed-semantics audit

## Decision

**REJECT the cached PDM dynamic terminal-speed field for H4 intent matching.** All 128
cached terminal dynamic-state speeds were exactly zero. In contrast, 119 anchors had a
position-derived final-segment average speed of at least `0.5 m/s`; 113 exceeded the
pre-registered `1.0 m/s` material-disagreement threshold. The maximum discrepancy was
`13.400592 m/s`.

## Implementation evidence

The audited NAVSIM PDM generator stores longitudinal speed in its internal IDM array while
the exported state array is initialized to zero and receives SE2 updates. The array-to-state
conversion faithfully copies the still-zero dynamic fields into `EgoState`. Source hashes
were captured in the raw result, and a payload-free audit replayed all 128 ledger rows and
the decision rule without failure.

## Consequence

R004H4P2's speed-gate result is not admissible evidence about the candidate provider. The
only authorized correction is a same-anchor, same-provider, same-threshold replay where
both native and challenger terminal speed are represented as rear-axle displacement over
the final `[3.5,4.0] s` interval divided by `0.5 s`. This is an interval-average speed, not
an instantaneous-speed claim.

No teacher, prediction, geometry, development, calibration, test or target data were read.
External cross-model review was unavailable and was not replaced by self-review.
