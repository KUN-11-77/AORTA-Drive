## What was tested

R004A tested only the source-data independence contract and the deterministic finite session time set. The official NAVSIM navtrain configuration contributed 1192 exported log fragments and 103288 eligible scene tokens. Fragments were grouped into 162 complete physical recordings before any predictor residual, model score, label outcome, or target-domain result was read.

The fixed allocation is 66 train, 24 development, 40 calibration, and 32 held-out source-test recordings. Allocation used only result-independent scene count, log count, recording month, and vehicle ID. The MILP reached its time limit with a valid incumbent; that incumbent was frozen by hash and is not represented as globally optimal.

## Results

- All 1192 logs and 103288 scenes occur exactly once.
- No recording group crosses a split.
- Scene-count imbalance from proportional targets is at most 0.1312%.
- Log-count imbalance from proportional targets is at most 0.3356%.
- Each recording contributes exactly 16 chronological midpoint-quantile anchors.
- The frozen time set contains 2592 unique scene tokens.
- The calibration split contains 40 independent sessions and 640 repeated within-session anchors.
- Forty sessions satisfy finite-threshold eligibility for the registered primary \(\alpha=0.10\) and secondary \(\alpha=0.05\).
- Two independent reconstructions were byte-identical to each other and to every committed manifest.

## Statistical interpretation

The calibration sample size is \(n=40\), not \(n=640\). Frames and exported fragments from the same physical recording are repeated measurements inside one session. All session-level risk and usefulness statements are restricted to the predeclared 16-point finite set \(T(Z)\); no claim is made for every time in a full recording.
