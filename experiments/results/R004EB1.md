## What was tested

R004E-B1 expanded the byte-replay-qualified visual cache to eight complete recording
groups without treating their 128 anchors as independent validation units. It generated
the frozen DINO dense maps, projection coordinates and explicit support masks, then joined
the resulting records to the already frozen R004E-A label manifest by exact
`(log_name, scene_token)` keys.

The 1536 anchor-slot references resolved to 1422 unique raw-image hashes. Content-addressed
deduplication therefore removed 114 duplicate image computations without merging distinct
image bytes or altering any anchor-slot reference.

## Results

- 8 recording groups and 128/128 expected anchors were present;
- 1422/1422 unique dense feature artifacts were reopened and validated;
- 256/256 projection arrays were reopened and validated;
- feature/projection hash mismatches, shape/finiteness failures and join failures were 0;
- all 128 expected label keys matched the frozen R004E-A manifest;
- one anchor per recording group was materialized on 64 deterministic nodes;
- NumPy versus torch bilinear sampling differed by at most
  `3.814697265625e-6`, below the pre-registered `1e-5` tolerance;
- invalid sampled slots were exact zero and all sampled values were finite;
- artifact size was 2,203,993,856 bytes, generation time 103.33 seconds and peak GPU
  allocation 2,034,846,720 bytes on the RTX 3090;
- calibration, source-test and target-domain samples were not accessed.

feature-label interface. It does not establish full 90-group completeness, training
throughput, learned accuracy, calibration, certificate utility, OOD robustness or Gate B
success. R004E-B2 may now build and independently audit all 1440 train/development
anchors under the unchanged protocol.
