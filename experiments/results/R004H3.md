that this does not imply a sharper **simultaneous session-tail envelope**: rare small learned
scales raise the joint maximum standardized residual, and the rank-max threshold expands
the median learned budget above uniform.

and has a lower minimum than uniform, so it could still help candidate footprints that use
well-allocated edges. Conversely, its large median and maximum may cause abstention. The
predeclared H4 common opportunity denominator must decide this without changing thresholds,
candidates or endpoints after seeing development.
