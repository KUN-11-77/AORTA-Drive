## What was tested

R004B tested only the source train/development camera-to-token interface. It audited all 1440 frozen anchors from 90 independent recording groups and 519 exported logs: three cameras, four history frames, and 4096 current-ego ground nodes per anchor. This produced 17,280 checked camera images. No calibration split, held-out source test, target appearance domain, road label, predictor residual, candidate outcome, or planner score was read.

The frozen projection applies the NAVSIM camera extrinsics, the OpenCV five-coefficient Brown--Conrady forward model, a bounded ideal-ray domain, a no-crop 1920×1080 to 512×288 half-pixel resize, and bilinear coordinates on the 18×32 DINO patch-center grid. Unsupported coordinates are not padded, clamped, or extrapolated.

## Results

- All images were 1920×1080; all time windows and files were present.
- The data contained 52 unique camera-calibration fingerprints.
- The NumPy projection and `cv2.projectPoints` had zero image-membership disagreements and a maximum bounded-domain absolute difference of (9.09\times10^{-13}) px.
- The maximum inverse-projected image-boundary radius was 0.93342; the minimum radial-only first turning radius was 1.14705. The frozen normalized-ray cap of 1.0 lies strictly between them.
- Ignoring lens distortion changed 2.289% of per-view node-support decisions on average and displaced supported nodes by 36.43 px / 0.607 patch on average; the maximum observed pixel displacement was 238.25 px.
- Excluding raw-image projections outside the patch-center convex hull removed 0.741% of all nodes per view on average, or 3.509% conditional on raw-image support. These nodes are deliberately marked missing.
- Current-frame three-camera support covered 46.41% of graph nodes on average. Four-frame support covered 58.43% on average, with 5th–95th percentiles 46.90%–71.10%. Therefore 41.57% of anchor-node instances still had no projective token support after all 12 slots.
- Frozen DINOv3-H+ accepted the exact 512×288 preprocessing and returned four finite normalized layer maps of shape `[1, 1280, 18, 32]`; their mean had the same shape. No training was performed.

## Architectural consequence

The visual interface is a masked 12-slot evidence tensor, not a dense fully observed BEV. Invalid slots may use zero only as a storage sentinel accompanied by the mask. Downstream aggregation must be masked; all-missing nodes require a dedicated learned missing token and an evidence-count feature. Hard best-view selection is prohibited. The mask denotes projective token support, not physical visibility: R004B does not model occlusion.
